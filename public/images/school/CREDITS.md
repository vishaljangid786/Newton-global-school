## Another school's branding — what was found and done

The supplied shoot mixes two schools on one campus. Alongside Newton Global
School there is **Tagore International School, Sangteda, Kotputli**, whose name
appears on pupils' embroidered polo crests, on a roadside hoarding, on one of
the buses, and on the reception banner.

- **Edited:** `reception.webp` — the Tagore crest at each end of the banner has
  been painted out and the Newton mark put in its place.
- **Cropped:** `computer-lab.webp` (a Tagore hoarding was visible through the
  right-hand window) and `founders-wall.webp` (the same banner at the edge).
- **Withdrawn:** twelve photographs whose subjects are Tagore pupils in crested
  uniform — library, computer lab, science lab, classroom, assembly and bus
  boarding. They are in `assets/unused-photos/` prefixed `other-school-`. The
  crest appears on every pupil in those frames, so it cannot be replaced
  convincingly, and the pupils are not Newton's.

That leaves no photograph of Newton pupils in a lesson, a lab or the library.
Those slots now use the rooms themselves, or the two Newton pupil photographs.
A short shoot of Newton pupils in the library, the labs and a classroom would
fill the gap.
